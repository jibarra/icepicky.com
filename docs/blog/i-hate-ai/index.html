I Hate AI

I hate that AI hallucinates. I hate that AI is not deterministic. I hate that AI adds friction to remain in control. I hate that AI is taking away people’s jobs.

I hate that AI pushes people to become generalists. I hate that AI causes people to forgo good practices. I hate that AI causes people to lack understanding. I hate that AI is eliminating the path from juniors to seniors. I hate that AI makes novices feel like experts.

I hate that AI makes it easy to not think. I hate that AI speaks definitively when not confident. I hate that AI causes people to just blindly accept output. I hate that AI makes it easy to not understand. I hate that AI strips people of their opinions and creativity. I hate that AI defaults to doing or answering instead of stopping for clarification.

I hate that AI causes people to act like it’s the answer to all their problems. I hate that AI’s success is being measured on output, instead of impact or outcomes. I hate that AI entices people to do everything because it feels so easy to do anything.

I hate that AI makes work less enjoyable. I hate that AI took away the joy of coding. I hate that AI sucked the fun out of solving issues. I hate that AI makes my expertise feel worthless.

I hate that AI makes everything gross. I hate that AI makes people feel bad. I hate that AI produces verbose documents. I hate that AI gets shoved into everything. I hate that AI makes people boring. I hate that AI is homogenizing our products.

I hate that AI makes us less connected and less human.
