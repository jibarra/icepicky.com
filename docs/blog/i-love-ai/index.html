I Love AI

I love that AI gives me quick answers. I love that AI enables me to explore new things. I love that AI resolves issues quickly. I love that AI is good at back and forth, like mock interviews.

I love that AI gives me space to do more. I love that AI gives me time to think at a high level. I love that AI helps me improve more things, like my local server. I love that AI helps me understand quicker. I love that AI does stuff that I didn’t have the time for before.

I love that AI helps people accomplish what they want. I love that AI allows non-coders to make code changes. I love that AI allows you to build the tool instead of buying it. I love that AI does the tedious, boring stuff without complaint. I love that AI removes friction to getting stuff done.

I love that AI raises the bar. I love that AI lifts the standard for good products and good businesses. I love that AI makes it more important to do the correct thing. I love that AI requires people to be accountable for what they do. I love that AI requires teams to work effectively. I love that AI challenges people to grow new skills.

I love that AI makes me better. I love that AI improved my code review skills. I love that AI gives me the magic “it’s amazing what computers can do” feeling. I love that AI shifted my viewpoint that a job is just a job.

I love that AI compels people to connect to each other more.
