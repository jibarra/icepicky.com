I love that AI gives me quick answers. I love that AI enables me to explore new things. I love that AI resolves issues quickly. I love that AI is good at back and forth, like mock interviews.

I love that AI gives me space to do more. I love that AI gives me time to think at a high level. I love that AI helps me improve more things, like my local server. I love that AI helps me understand quicker. I love that AI [does stuff that I didn't have the time for before.](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/refactoring-economic-benefit.html)

I love that AI helps people accomplish what they want. I love that AI allows non-coders to make code changes. I love that AI allows you to [build the tool instead of buying it.](https://posthog.com/blog/sql-parser) I love that AI does the tedious, boring stuff without complaint. I love that AI removes friction to getting stuff done.

I love that AI raises the bar. I love that AI [lifts the standard for good products and good businesses.](https://ogirardot.writizzy.blog/p/good-software-knows-when-to-stop) I love that AI [makes it more important to do the correct thing.](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/the-cost-yagni-was-never-about) I love that AI [requires people to be accountable for what they do.](https://www.ashbyhq.com/blog/engineering/ai-ashby-engineering-and-the-future) I love that AI [requires teams to work effectively.](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/7/13/the-tower-keeps-rising/) I love that AI [challenges people to grow new skills.](https://charity.wtf/p/ai-demands-more-engineering-discipline)

I love that AI makes me better. I love that AI improved my code review skills. I love that AI [gives me the magic "it's amazing what computers can do" feeling.](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47282777) I love that AI [shifted my viewpoint that a job is just a job.](https://jry.io/writing/you-are-not-your-job/)

I love that AI compels people to connect to each other more.
