I hate that AI hallucinates. I hate that AI is not deterministic. I hate that AI adds friction to remain in control. [I hate that AI is taking away people's jobs.](https://tomtunguz.com/marginal-hire/)

I hate that AI [pushes people to become generalists.](https://bonusly.com/post/maybe-this-everyones-a-builder-thing-is-wrong) I hate that AI [causes people to forgo good practices.](https://newsletter.kentbeck.com/p/the-cost-yagni-was-never-about) I hate that AI causes people to lack understanding. I hate that AI [is eliminating the path from juniors to seniors.](https://nmn.gl/blog/ai-and-learning) I hate that AI [makes novices feel like experts.](https://correresmidestino.com/dont-you-just-upload-it-to-chatgpt/)

I hate that AI makes it easy to not think. I hate that AI speaks definitively when not confident. I hate that AI causes people to just blindly accept output. I hate that AI [makes it easy to not understand.](https://www.artfish.ai/p/offloading-thinking-to-ai) I hate that AI [strips people of their opinions and creativity.](https://adiamond.me/2026/06/software-engineering-in-the-age-of-ai/) I hate that AI defaults to doing or answering instead of stopping for clarification.

I hate that AI causes people to act like it's the answer to all their problems. I hate that AI's success is being measured on output, instead of impact or outcomes. I hate that AI [entices people to do everything because it feels so easy to do anything.](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/7/13/the-tower-keeps-rising/)

I hate that AI makes work less enjoyable. I hate that AI took away the joy of coding. I hate that AI sucked the fun out of solving issues. I hate that AI makes my expertise feel worthless.

I hate that AI [makes everything gross.](https://www.alecscollon.com/blog/llm-burnout/) I hate that AI [makes people feel bad.](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/what-engineers-feeling-right-now-why-we-arent-talking-scott-walker-qyirc/) I hate that AI [produces verbose documents.](https://vickiboykis.com/2026/08/12/write-for-people/) I hate that AI [gets shoved into everything.](https://blog.mathieui.net/this-is-not-the-future.html) I hate that AI [makes people boring.](https://www.marginalia.nu/log/a_132_ai_bores/) I hate that AI is homogenizing our products.

I hate that AI makes us less connected and less human.
