My advice is a happy and healthy family makes a happy and healthy baby. Every one will have advice but only you will know what your family needs. So it's okay to ignore unsolicited advice and do what's best for the three of you. There's only one family shaped the way you are so only you will know what's best.

Good luck and enjoy every day with your new baby!
